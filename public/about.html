WHO WE ARE

Christian artistry.
Created with purpose.

Nonrev Christian Music is a Christian music publishing and label service company. Through original production, music publishing and artist development, we support Christian creatives as they bring meaningful music projects to life.

Our mission is to empower Christian artistry to reach its creative purpose unrestricted, while releasing inspiring music that edifies the Church and glorifies Jesus Christ.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE MUSIC
MEET THE FOUNDER

Vernon Hill.

FOUNDER · PRODUCER · SONGWRITER

Born and raised in Maryland, Vernon Hill began playing piano by ear at age five. His musical path led him to study music production and to serve in worship ministry, where he developed a passion for songs that bring people into a deeper relationship with God.

Vernon founded Nonrev Christian Music to bring artists and worship leaders together through original songwriting, music production and publishing. Projects include Songs from His Presence, Unshaken and Great Joy.
