Full Worship EP “Unshaken” Presented By: Dynamic Music Producer/Songwriter Vernon Hill Released to C

Feb 23, 2016

Music Producer and Songwriter Vernon Hill, presents his brand new Worship EP “Unshaken”. This inspirational, soul-stirring 4 song worship EP features the singles title “Give You Glory” featuring Taylor-Simone-Butts (which will be released to radio in May), “Need the Savior” featuring LaVarn Gordon (follow-up single which will be released to radio in September) and “Unshaken”. His current single “Heart & Soul” which features Keon Jones is picking up momentum with Billboard Christian Soft AC Most Added Chart and with rotation on FaithRadio WLBF.
