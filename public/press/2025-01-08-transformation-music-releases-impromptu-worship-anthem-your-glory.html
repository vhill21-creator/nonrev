Transformation Music Releases Impromptu Worship Anthem, "Your Glory"

Jan 8, 2025

Transformation Music, the worship ministry of Transformation Christian Fellowship (TCF), is excited to announce the release of a brand-new worship anthem titled “Your Glory.”  This spontaneous song, captured live during a Sunday worship experience led by Senior Pastor Brandon Hill , is already resonating with the hearts of the congregation and beyond. “Your Glory” emerged organically during an intense moment of worship, as the presence of God swept through the sanctuary. Pastor Brandon Hill shared, “This song is more than just lyrics and melody; it’s a heartfelt declaration of the majesty and power of God that we experienced together as a church family. It’s a reminder of why we worship—to lift up His glory.” The live recording preserves the authentic and raw atmosphere of the service, bringing listeners into the sacred moment as it unfolded. This release is just the beginning for Transformation Music, which is gearing up to launch multiple singles  in 2025 to elevate and enhance the worship experience at TCF and for the global church community. “Your Glory”  will be available on all major streaming platforms soon, inviting listeners to step into the worship experience that ignited a new anthem for the church. For updates, follow Transformation Music  and Transformation Christian Fellowship  on social media or visit http://www.transformationcf.org/music
