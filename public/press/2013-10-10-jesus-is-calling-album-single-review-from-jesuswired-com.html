"Jesus Is Calling" Album Single Review from JesusWired.com

Oct 10, 2013

Acclaimed independent Maryland musician, Vernon Hill, released his debut CD, “Songs from His Presence,” September 17th. The first single from the album is entitled, “Jesus Is Calling.” From its beginning piano intro, “Jesus Is Calling” catches your heart and attention. With smooth vocals, uncomplicated lyrics, and tight production, Hill implores people to come to Jesus just as they are. Come just as you are/ Lay it all at his feet Jesus is calling/ Jesus is calling You who are heavy laden/ Come unto Him/ And He’ll give you rest He is calling/ Jesus is calling/ Hear Him call you/ Feel Him draw you The song is a joy to listen to. In fact, you’ll find yourself doing just that, over and over again, enveloped in God’s presence.
