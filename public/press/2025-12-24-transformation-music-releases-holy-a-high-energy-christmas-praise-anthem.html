Transformation Music Releases “Holy” — A High-Energy Christmas Praise Anthem for Radio & Worship Audiences

Dec 24, 2025

Transformation Music, the worship team of Transformation Christian Fellowship , announces the release of their new single “Holy,”  a fast, mid-tempo Christmas praise anthem filled with joy, energy, and worship. Crafted to resonate with radio audiences and congregations alike, “Holy” delivers an uplifting sound that celebrates the holiness of God while capturing the joyful spirit of the Christmas season. Designed for everything from holiday road trips and family celebrations to church worship playlists, “Holy” blends vibrant rhythms with praise-driven lyrics—making it a song that carries Christmas joy while remaining relevant throughout the year. 🎄🙌 Joy-filled. Faith-lifting. Praise-driven. The single is produced by Vernon Hill , an accomplished music producer known for impactful worship releases including “Jesus Is Calling”  and “Unshaken,”  both of which gained traction on Billboard Soft AC Radio charts  and reached international and SiriusXM Gospel Radio airwaves . Hill also produced “God of Wonders”  by Otisia Hill, which has surpassed 10,000 Spotify streams , continuing his track record of producing music that connects across platforms and audiences. “I’m really excited to share this song of praise for this Christmas season,”  says producer Vernon Hill . “But it’s also meant to glorify God throughout the year by pointing people to the character and holiness of God.” With its upbeat tempo, celebratory sound, and worship-centered message, “Holy” is positioned as a strong addition to Christmas and Gospel radio rotations, while also serving churches and listeners seeking joyful, Christ-centered praise beyond the holiday season. “Holy” is available now on all major digital music platforms. iTunes | Apple Music | Spotify
